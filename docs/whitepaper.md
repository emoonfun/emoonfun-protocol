# EMO Whitepaper

## Vision and mission

**Vision.** Make a token's growth something you can watch, not just a number that moves.

**Mission.** Give every launch a character, a ladder of forms and a shared treasury, so that each milestone is visible, backed and remembered.

EMO connects token launches on Solana with a progression system inspired by creature-evolution games. Market-cap milestones become moments worth following together.

## How it works

1. A token is created and paired with a character. It begins in its base form.
2. When the token reaches the second threshold (currently stated as $10K market cap), its card updates to the second form and the treasury buys the token.
3. At the final threshold (currently stated as $30K market cap) the character reaches its final form and the treasury buys again.
4. Treasury purchases are locked for 30 days.
5. When the token graduates from its bonding curve, a further purchase is made; its size is still to be announced.

## Roadmap

**Phase 1: Foundation.** Brand, website with live market board, contract address and social links published once they exist.

**Phase 2: Launch.** Token goes live on pump.fun, treasury wallet is published, first characters are revealed.

**Phase 3: Evolution.** Threshold tracking goes live, forms update on card pages, treasury purchases and locks are visible to everyone.

**Phase 4: Expansion.** More characters, community participation features, and public documentation of the treasury.
